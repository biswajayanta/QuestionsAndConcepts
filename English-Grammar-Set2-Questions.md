# Class 10 English Grammar — Set 2 (Genuine CBSE Exam Format)
### Determiners | Tenses | Modals | Subject–Verb Concord | Reported Speech

*This set drops MCQs and assertion-reasoning entirely and matches the actual CBSE paper format: gap-fill inside a passage/dialogue (no options given — he has to supply the word himself), editing/error-correction, and dialogue-to-reported-speech conversion. This is exactly how these units are tested in the real exam.*

## UNIT 1 — DETERMINERS (Gap-fill in a passage — fill each blank with ONE appropriate determiner)

Read the passage and fill in each blank with an appropriate determiner.

> Ravi is (a) _____ honest and hardworking boy. He has (b) _____ friends in his new school, since he joined only last month, but the few he has are close ones. Every morning, he wakes up at (c) _____ same time and goes for a run. He doesn't have (d) _____ money to spare, but he still gives (e) _____ little to charity every month. (f) _____ students in his class admire him for this habit, though a few think he should save more for himself. Yesterday, he saw (g) _____ old man struggling to cross the road, and he helped him without a second thought. (h) _____ act of kindness earned him praise from his teacher. He wants to be (i) _____ engineer one day, and he knows (j) _____ hour of study every single day, without fail, will help him get there.

*(10 blanks — write the determiner he needs for each position, labelled (a) to (j).)*

## UNIT 2 — TENSES (Gap-fill in a passage — use the correct tense of the verb given in brackets)

> Every year, our school (a) _____ (organise) an annual sports day. This year, the event (b) _____ (take place) next Friday. Students (c) _____ (already/start) practising for their events. Yesterday, while the coach (d) _____ (train) the athletics team, it suddenly (e) _____ (start) raining, and the practice (f) _____ (have) to be cancelled. By the time the rain stopped, most students (g) _____ (leave) for home. My friend Aisha (h) _____ (participate) in the school sports day every year since Class 6, and she (i) _____ (win) the 100m race three times so far. This year, she (j) _____ (hope) to win it again.

*(10 blanks — write the correct tense form for each verb given in brackets.)*

## UNIT 3 — MODALS (Gap-fill in a passage — fill each blank with an appropriate modal)

> Students (a) _____ always submit their assignments on time; it is a firm school rule. However, if a student is genuinely unwell, they (b) _____ inform the class teacher in advance, though they are not required to give a written explanation unless asked. A student (c) _____ not enter the laboratory without the teacher's permission — this rule is strictly enforced for safety reasons. "(d) _____ I leave the room for a moment, Ma'am?" asked Reena politely. During examinations, students (e) _____ not talk to one another under any circumstances. If it rains heavily, the sports day (f) _____ be postponed — the school hasn't decided yet. When I was younger, I (g) _____ solve arithmetic problems very quickly, though I've grown a bit rusty now. "(h) _____ you please help me carry these books to the staff room?" the teacher asked a student.

## UNIT 5 — REPORTED SPEECH (Dialogue completion — the actual CBSE exam format)

**Part A.** Read the dialogue between a teacher and a student, then complete the reported speech passage that follows.

> **Teacher:** Why were you absent yesterday?
> **Sohan:** I was not well, Ma'am. I had a high fever.
> **Teacher:** You should have informed the school office.
> **Sohan:** I am sorry, Ma'am. I will inform them next time.
> **Teacher:** Please submit a medical certificate tomorrow.
> **Sohan:** Sure, Ma'am. I will bring it without fail.

Complete the passage below using the dialogue above:

> The teacher asked Sohan (a) _____________________________. Sohan replied that (b) _____________________________ and that (c) _____________________________. The teacher told him that (d) _____________________________. Sohan apologised and said that (e) _____________________________. The teacher then requested him (f) _____________________________. Sohan assured her that (g) _____________________________.

**Part B.** Convert the following dialogue into a reported speech paragraph (write it out fully yourself — don't just fill blanks this time).

> **Mother:** Where have you kept my umbrella?
> **Riya:** I kept it in the hall cupboard, Mom.
> **Mother:** Please bring it to me; it looks like it's going to rain.
> **Riya:** Okay, Mom. I will bring it right away.
> **Mother:** Don't forget to take your raincoat as well when you leave for school.
> **Riya:** I won't forget. Thank you for reminding me.

*(Write the full reported-speech version of this dialogue as a single connected passage, similar to Part A.)*
