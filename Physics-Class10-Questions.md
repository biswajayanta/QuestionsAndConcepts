## How to read the "Expected answer" line under each question

Each question carries an *Expected answer* line telling you the length, and whether a diagram, formula, law statement or numerical working is needed. Use it to structure the answer and to check the format afterwards.

| Marks | Typical length | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| MCQ / Assertion-Reason (1) | Option letter only | No working is marked. |
| Very short (2) | 2 to 4 lines, 30 to 50 words | One mark per correct point. |
| Short (3) | 5 to 7 lines, 50 to 80 words | Three clear points, or a diagram plus two points. |
| Long (5) | 10 to 15 lines, 100 to 150 words | Answer each lettered part separately. |
| Case-based (4) | 1 to 2 lines per part | Answer only what each part asks. |

**Always:**
- **Diagrams:** pencil and ruler, arrows on field lines and rays, every label written.
- **Numericals:** write the formula, substitute with units, and give the final answer with its unit.
- **Law or rule statements:** one complete sentence.

# MAGNETIC EFFECTS OF ELECTRIC CURRENT (MG1 to MG12)

### MCQ (1 mark each)

**MG1.** Outside a bar magnet, the magnetic field lines:
(a) emerge from the south pole and enter the north pole
(b) emerge from the north pole and enter the south pole
(c) cross each other near the poles
(d) are straight lines parallel to the magnet's axis
*Expected answer: option letter only.*

**MG2.** The magnetic field produced at a point near a straight current-carrying wire becomes stronger when:
(a) the distance of the point from the wire is increased
(b) the direction of the current is reversed
(c) the current through the wire is increased
(d) the point is taken to the opposite side of the wire
*Expected answer: option letter only.*

**MG3.** In Fleming's left-hand rule, the forefinger points along the magnetic field and the central finger along the current. The thumb points along:
(a) the force on the conductor
(b) the induced current
(c) the field produced by the conductor
(d) the motion of the magnet
*Expected answer: option letter only.*

### Assertion-Reason (1 mark each)

**MG4.** **Assertion (A):** No two magnetic field lines intersect each other.
**Reason (R):** Magnetic field lines are closed curves.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.
*Expected answer: option letter only.*

**MG5.** **Assertion (A):** In India, the alternating current supplied to homes reverses its direction 50 times every second.
**Reason (R):** The frequency of the AC supply in India is 50 Hz.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

### Case-based (4 marks each)

# ELECTRICITY (EL1 to EL7)

(a) R/2  (b) R  (c) 2R  (d) 4R
*Expected answer: option letter only.*

**Reason (R):** Copper has a high resistivity.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

**EL3. (2 marks)** Define electrical resistivity. State its SI unit and name one alloy that has a high resistivity.
*Expected answer: a one-sentence definition [1], the unit and one alloy [1]. About 3 lines.*

**EL4. (3 marks)** Two resistors of 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in parallel, and this combination is connected in series with a 1.6 Ω resistor and a 12 V battery. Find (a) the total resistance of the circuit, (b) the current drawn from the battery, and (c) the current through the 6 Ω resistor.
*Expected answer: a numerical. Write the formula, substitute with units, and give each answer with its unit. A circuit diagram is optional. About 6 lines of working.*

**EL5. (3 marks)** An electric heater is rated 1100 W, 220 V. Find (a) the current it draws, (b) its resistance, and (c) the energy it consumes in 2 hours in kWh, and the cost at ₹5 per kWh.

**EL6. (5 marks)**

(d) State Joule's law of heating, with the formula. **[1]**
*Expected answer: 10 to 12 lines in all. (a) the law as one sentence. (b) a labelled circuit diagram with standard symbols (cell or battery, key, ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel, resistor, rheostat). (c) a labelled graph with axes and one line. (d) one sentence and the formula.*

**EL7. (4 marks, case-based)** The table gives the resistivity of some materials at 20 °C.
