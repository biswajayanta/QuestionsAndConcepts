## Physics — Lenses (the priority one)

**P4R-1.**
An object 5 cm tall is placed 40 cm from a convex lens of focal length 20 cm.
(a) Find the image distance using the lens formula.
(b) State the magnification using the correct lens formula for magnification (write the formula you're using explicitly).
(c) Based on your answers, state clearly: is the image real or virtual? Erect or inverted? Enlarged, same size, or diminished?
(d) In one sentence, explain how you decided real vs. virtual from the sign of v — don't just state the answer, state the *rule* you used.

**P4R-2.**
An object 5 cm tall is placed 10 cm from the same convex lens (focal length 20 cm — object is now *inside* the focal length).
(a) Find the image distance using the lens formula.
(b) State the magnification (again, write the formula explicitly).
(c) Is this image real or virtual? Erect or inverted? Enlarged, same size, or diminished?
(d) Compare your sign-convention reasoning here to P4R-1 — what changed, and why did that flip the answer?

## Biology — Tropisms

**B3R-1.**

(a) Predict what the stem will do over the next few days, and name the tropism responsible.

## Biology — Excretion

**B4R-1.**
Two people, A (healthy) and B (diabetic, untreated), both have glucose filtered at the glomerulus into their nephron tubules. Person A's urine has no detectable glucose; Person B's urine does.
Explain this difference by describing what happens at the **reabsorption** step for each person — specifically, address what determines whether all the filtered glucose gets reabsorbed back into the blood or not. Your answer should explain *why* the amount of glucose filtered matters, not just restate that B has diabetes.
