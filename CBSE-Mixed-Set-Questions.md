# TOPIC 1: TRIANGLES

**T1. (1 mark)** △ABC ~ △DEF. If AB = 6 cm, DE = 9 cm and ar(△ABC) = 36 cm², then ar(△DEF) is:
(a) 54 cm²  (b) 81 cm²  (c) 24 cm²  (d) 16 cm²

**T2. (1 mark)** **Assertion (A):** All congruent triangles are similar.
**Reason (R):** All similar triangles are congruent.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

**T3. (2 marks)** The perimeters of two similar triangles are 24 cm and 36 cm. Find (a) the ratio of their corresponding sides, and (b) the ratio of their areas.

**T4. (2 marks)** The sides of a triangle are 7 cm, 24 cm and 25 cm. Is it a right-angled triangle? Name the theorem that justifies your answer.

**T5. (3 marks)** In △ABC, DE ∥ BC, where D is on AB and E is on AC. If AD = x, DB = x − 2, AE = x + 2 and EC = x − 1, find the value of x.

**T6. (3 marks)** ABCD is a trapezium with AB ∥ DC. Its diagonals AC and BD intersect at O. Show that AO/OC = BO/OD.

**T7. (5 marks)** Prove that the ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.

**T8. (4 marks, case-based)** At the same time of the day, a vertical pole 6 m high casts a shadow 8 m long on level ground. A vertical tower beside it casts a shadow 40 m long.
(i) Name the similarity criterion that makes the two triangles formed by the objects and their shadows similar, and say why it applies. **[1]**
(ii) Find the height of the tower. **[1]**
(iii) Find the distance from the top of the tower to the tip of its shadow. **[1]**
(iv) Find the ratio of the area of the triangle formed by the pole to the area of the triangle formed by the tower. **[1]**

# TOPIC 2: CIRCLES

**C1. (1 mark)** The length of the tangent drawn from a point 5 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 3 cm is:
(a) 2 cm  (b) 4 cm  (c) 8 cm  (d) √34 cm

**C2. (1 mark)** **Assertion (A):** From a point inside a circle, exactly two tangents can be drawn to the circle.
**Reason (R):** A tangent to a circle touches it at exactly one point.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

**C3. (2 marks)** Two tangents PA and PB are drawn from an external point P to a circle with centre O, and they are inclined to each other at 80°. Find ∠POA.

**C4. (2 marks)** From a point Q, the length of the tangent to a circle is 24 cm, and the distance of Q from the centre is 25 cm. Find the radius of the circle.

**C5. (3 marks)** A quadrilateral ABCD is drawn to circumscribe a circle. Prove that AB + CD = AD + BC.

**C6. (3 marks)** A circle is inscribed in △ABC and touches the sides BC, CA and AB at D, E and F respectively. If AB = 12 cm, BC = 8 cm and CA = 10 cm, find AF, BD and CE.

**C7. (5 marks)**
(a) Prove that the tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact. **[3]**
(b) A tangent of length 8 cm is drawn from a point 10 cm from the centre of a circle. Find the radius. **[2]**

**C8. (4 marks, case-based)** A circular park has radius 20 m and centre O. A gate P lies outside the park, 29 m from O. Two straight paths PA and PB touch the park's boundary at points A and B.
(i) What is the measure of ∠OAP? Give the reason. **[1]**
(ii) Find the length of the path PA. **[1]**
(iii) Find the area of quadrilateral OAPB. **[1]**
(iv) Find the total length of the route from A to P to B. **[1]**

# TOPIC 3: ELECTRICITY

**E1. (1 mark)** Two resistors of 4 Ω and 12 Ω are connected in parallel. The equivalent resistance is:
(a) 16 Ω  (b) 8 Ω  (c) 3 Ω  (d) 48 Ω

**E2. (1 mark)** **Assertion (A):** Domestic appliances are connected in parallel with the supply.
**Reason (R):** In a parallel connection each appliance gets the same potential difference and can be switched on or off independently.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

**E3. (2 marks)** An electric bulb connected to a 220 V supply draws a current of 0.5 A. Calculate its power and its resistance.

**E4. (2 marks)** Give two reasons why a series arrangement is not used for connecting electrical appliances in a domestic circuit.

**E5. (3 marks)** A 12 V battery is connected to a 2 Ω resistor in series with a parallel combination of a 6 Ω and a 3 Ω resistor. Find (a) the total resistance of the circuit, (b) the current drawn from the battery, and (c) the current through the 6 Ω resistor.

**E6. (3 marks)** A house has three 60 W bulbs, each used for 5 hours a day. Find (a) the energy consumed in 30 days, in kWh, and (b) the cost at ₹6 per kWh.

**E7. (5 marks)**
(a) Derive the expression for the equivalent resistance of two resistors R1 and R2 connected in series. **[3]**
(b) Three resistors of 2 Ω, 3 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in parallel across a 6 V battery. Find the total resistance and the total current. **[2]**

**E8. (4 marks, case-based)** A student connects a conductor in a circuit with a battery, an ammeter and a voltmeter, and records:

| Potential difference V (volt) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current I (ampere) | 0.2 | 0.4 | 0.6 | 0.8 |

(i) Which law does this experiment verify? **[1]**
(ii) What is the shape of the V–I graph? **[1]**
(iii) Find the resistance of the conductor. **[1]**
(iv) The same material and thickness of wire is used, but its length is doubled. What is the new resistance, and what current flows at 4 V? **[1]**

**K3. (2 marks)** Name the functional group present in each: (a) CH3CHO  (b) CH3COOH  (c) CH3CH2OH  (d) CH3COCH3. Also name the class of compounds each belongs to.

**K4. (2 marks)** What are isomers? Draw the structural formulae of two isomers of butane (C4H10).

**K5. (3 marks)**
(a) Write the chemical equation for the reaction between ethanol and ethanoic acid in the presence of conc. H2SO4. Name the product and state its smell. **[2]**

**K6. (3 marks)** Explain the cleansing action of soap. Include micelle formation in your answer.

(b) ethanoic acid reacting with sodium carbonate

**K8. (4 marks, case-based)** A student studies the series of alcohols: methanol (CH3OH), ethanol (C2H5OH), propanol (C3H7OH) and butanol (C4H9OH).
(i) Name the functional group and the general formula of this series. **[1]**
(ii) What is the difference in structure and in molecular mass between two successive members? **[1]**

# TOPIC 5: HEREDITY

**H1. (1 mark)** In a cross of Tt × Tt in pea plants, 400 plants are obtained. The expected number of dwarf plants is:
(a) 100  (b) 200  (c) 300  (d) 400

**H2. (1 mark)** **Assertion (A):** The sex of a human child is determined by the mother's chromosomes.
**Reason (R):** Every human egg carries only the X chromosome.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

**H3. (2 marks)** Differentiate between genotype and phenotype, with one example.

**H4. (2 marks)** Explain how the number of chromosomes is kept the same from one human generation to the next. Give the numbers in your answer.

**H5. (3 marks)** Pure round-seeded pea plants (RR) are crossed with pure wrinkled-seeded plants (rr). Show the cross as a flow diagram up to the F2 generation, starting from the gametes, and state the phenotypic ratio of the F2.

**H6. (3 marks)** A couple has two sons and wishes for a daughter.
(a) Which parent determines the sex of the child? **[1]**
(b) Show with a flow diagram, starting from the gametes of the parents, how a boy or a girl is born. **[1]**
(c) What is the probability that their next child is a girl? **[1]**

**H7. (5 marks)** A pure round yellow-seeded pea plant (RRYY) is crossed with a pure wrinkled green-seeded plant (rryy).
(a) State the genotype and phenotype of the F1. **[1]**
(b) Name the four kinds of gametes formed by the F1. **[1]**
(c) State the phenotypes and the ratio in the F2. **[1]**
(d) If 640 F2 plants are obtained, how many are in each phenotypic class? **[1]**
(e) What does the appearance of round green and wrinkled yellow seeds in the F2 show? **[1]**

**H8. (4 marks, case-based)** A student crosses pure violet-flowered pea plants (VV) with white-flowered plants (vv). All the F1 plants have violet flowers. The F1 plants are self-pollinated and 800 F2 plants are obtained.
(i) Write the genotype and phenotype of the F1 plants. **[1]**
(ii) Which flower colour is dominant? How do you know? **[1]**
(iii) How many white-flowered plants and how many pure violet (VV) plants are expected in the F2? **[1]**
(iv) A white-flowered F2 plant is self-pollinated. What colour are the flowers of its offspring? Give a reason. **[1]**
