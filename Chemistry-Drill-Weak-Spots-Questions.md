# Chemistry Drill: Three Weak Spots
### Class 10 CBSE | NCERT scope only | 20 marks | about 25 minutes

This drill targets the habits that cost marks in the Chemistry practice set:
1. Giving a weak or wrong **"why"** step.
2. Putting the wrong **condition or reagent** on an equation, or naming the wrong **type**.
3. Drawing **structures** with the wrong number of bonds.

*Write the answers in your notebook, then send a photo for review. No hints are given here.*

---

## PART A: The "why" step (8 marks)

Answer each in **one sentence that starts with "because"**. The sentence must name the actual cause (a force, an ion, an electron, an energy). Restating the question earns nothing.

**A1.** Why does carbon form covalent bonds instead of ionic bonds?
*Expected answer: one sentence, naming what happens to the electrons and why it is not possible.*

**A2.** Why does the boiling point rise as you go down a homologous series?
*Expected answer: one sentence. Name what grows with molecular mass.*

**A3.** Why do detergents work in hard water when soaps do not?
*Expected answer: one sentence about the calcium and magnesium salts of each.*

**A4.** Why are sodium and potassium kept in kerosene?
*Expected answer: one sentence with two parts: what they react with, and what kerosene does.*

**A5.** Why does sodium chloride have a high melting point?
*Expected answer: one sentence. Name the force and say why it matters.*

**A6.** Why does solid sodium chloride not conduct electricity, but molten sodium chloride does?
*Expected answer: one sentence about the ions in each state.*

**A7.** Why can sodium not be extracted from its oxide by heating with carbon?
*Expected answer: one sentence comparing sodium and carbon.*

**A8.** Why does ethene undergo addition reactions but ethane does not?
*Expected answer: one sentence naming the bond that makes the difference.*

## PART B: Conditions and types (8 marks)

For each, write a **balanced equation with the condition or reagent on the arrow** (write "no catalyst needed" if none is required), then name the **type of reaction**. Name the organic product in words where there is one.

*Marking: ½ for the correct balanced equation with the correct condition, ½ for the type.*

**B1.** Ethanoic acid + sodium carbonate
*Expected answer: one equation, products named, type.*

**B2.** Ethanol → ethanoic acid
*Expected answer: one equation with the reagent on the arrow, type.*

**B3.** Ethanol → ethene
*Expected answer: one equation with the reagent and temperature on the arrow, type.*

**B4.** Ethanoic acid + ethanol
*Expected answer: one equation with the catalyst on the arrow, name of the product, type.*

**B5.** Ethene + hydrogen
*Expected answer: one equation with the catalyst on the arrow, type.*

**B6.** Methane + chlorine → chloromethane
*Expected answer: one equation with the condition on the arrow, type.*

**B7.** Zinc sulphide heated strongly in excess air
*Expected answer: one balanced equation, name of the process.*

**B8.** Ethyl ethanoate + sodium hydroxide
*Expected answer: one equation, products named, name of the reaction.*

## PART C: Drawing structures (4 marks)

Draw the **full structural formula with every bond shown**. Mark the functional group. **Before you finish, count the bonds:** every carbon has 4, every hydrogen has 1, every oxygen has 2.

*Marking: 1 mark each, for a correct structure with the group marked.*

**C1.** Ethanoic acid (mark the functional group).
*Expected answer: one drawing with all bonds shown.*

**C2.** Propanone (mark the functional group).
*Expected answer: one drawing with all bonds shown.*

**C3.** Propanal (mark the functional group).
*Expected answer: one drawing with all bonds shown.*

**C4.** The two isomers of butane (C4H10).
*Expected answer: two drawings, one for each isomer, with all bonds shown.*
