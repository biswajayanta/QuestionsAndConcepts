## Mathematics

**M1. (Polynomials & Real Numbers)**
Let α and β be the roots of the equation x² − px + q = 0, where p and q are positive integers. It is given that:
- α² + β² = 58
- αβ is a prime number less than 10

Find the values of p and q. Show that your answer is the *only* possibility consistent with both conditions.

**M2. (Trigonometry — Heights & Distances)**
The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point A on level ground is θ. You then walk a distance *d* directly towards the tower, reaching point B, where the angle of elevation becomes (90° − θ).

(a) Show that the height of the tower is:
h = d / (cot θ − tan θ)

(b) Hence find the height of the tower when d = 20 m and θ = 30°.

## Physics

**P1. (Refraction through a glass slab)**
A ray of light strikes a rectangular glass slab of thickness 6 cm at an angle of incidence of 60°. The refractive index of the glass is √3.

(a) Find the angle of refraction inside the slab.
(b) Find the **lateral displacement** of the emergent ray (i.e., the perpendicular distance between the path of the original incident ray, extended, and the actual emergent ray).

**P2. (Concave mirror — magnification)**
An object is placed in front of a concave mirror of focal length 15 cm. The image formed is **three times the size of the object**.

Find **all possible** object distances from the mirror, and state for each case whether the image is real or virtual, and where it forms. (Careful: "three times the size" does not by itself tell you whether the image is real or virtual — that's exactly the point of this question.)

## Chemistry

**C1. (Reactivity & amphoteric behaviour)**
A silvery-white metal X:
- reacts with dilute HCl to liberate a colourless, odourless gas that burns with a "pop" sound, and
- *also* reacts with NaOH solution to liberate the same gas.

(a) Identify metal X.
(b) Write balanced chemical equations for both reactions.
(c) Most common metals do *not* react with a base. Explain, in your own words, why X is an exception.

**C2. (pH, neutralisation, and strength vs. concentration)**
Three solutions, A, B, and C, have pH values of 4, 7, and 10 respectively.
- A turns blue litmus red.
- C turns red litmus blue.
- B affects neither.

Equal volumes of A and C are mixed, and the resulting mixture has a pH of exactly 7.

(a) Name the type of reaction that has occurred, and write a general equation for it.
(b) What does the *final pH of exactly 7* tell you about the relative strength/concentration of the acid in A compared to the base in C? Explain your reasoning — don't just state a rule.

## Biology

**B1. (Life Processes — respiration)**
During intense exercise, oxygen supply to human muscle cells can become insufficient, and muscle cells switch to a different metabolic pathway — which is also why you feel a burning sensation/cramp in your muscles afterwards.

(a) Name this pathway and its end product in human muscle cells.
(b) Yeast cells, when short of oxygen, also switch pathways — but produce different end products. Name them.
(c) Explain, in terms of approximate ATP yield per glucose molecule, why aerobic respiration is far more efficient than either of the above.

**B2. (Control & Coordination — reflex arc)**
When a doctor taps just below your kneecap, your leg kicks up involuntarily — and this happens *before* you consciously register that you were tapped.

(a) Name, in the correct sequence, the components of the reflex arc involved in this action.
(b) Explain why this response is faster than a voluntary action (like catching a thrown ball), in terms of the neural pathway each one takes.
(c) If one specific part of this reflex arc were damaged, the leg would still kick — but the person would never consciously *feel* the tap. Which part is that, and why?

## Social Science (History)

**H1. (Nationalism in Europe)**
Between 1815 (the fall of Napoleon) and 1848, conservative governments across Europe tried to restore the old order — yet liberal and nationalist movements kept challenging them throughout this period.

Using **two specific examples** of events or movements from this period, explain: how did conservative forces typically respond when a nationalist/liberal uprising occurred, and what had changed in the balance between conservatism and nationalism by around 1848?

**H2. (Age of Industrialisation)**
It would seem obvious that Indian handloom weavers and craftspeople would simply be wiped out by cheap, machine-made British textiles in the 19th century. Yet in certain periods and for certain products, handicraft producers survived — and in some cases even did better.

Give **two distinct reasons** (not two examples of the same reason) that explain this survival, and for each, explain the underlying economic logic — not just what happened, but *why* it happened.
